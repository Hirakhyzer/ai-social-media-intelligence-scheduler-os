# Ethical Marketing Boundary

PostPilot OS is an independent synthetic research prototype for studying social-media intelligence, planning, scheduling, and analytics workflows.

## Intended use

Acceptable uses include:

- Teaching ethical marketing automation and content operations.
- Simulating trend radar, platform-fit scoring, best-time recommendations, and content calendars.
- Building human-reviewed content planning workflows.
- Testing brand-safety, claim-safety, spam-risk, and auditability controls.
- Exporting synthetic reports, figures, and calendar plans for research.

## Non-intended use

This project is not intended for:

- Spam, mass unsolicited posting, or automated engagement farming.
- Fake likes, fake comments, fake reviews, or artificial amplification.
- Impersonation, deception, astroturfing, or synthetic identity operations.
- Scraping private personal data or bypassing platform rules.
- Targeting vulnerable people or sensitive attributes.
- Political persuasion targeting or manipulation campaigns.
- Posting to real accounts without explicit human approval and platform compliance.

## Human approval gate

The default pipeline is simulation-only. Any real posting adapter must include:

- Clear credential handling.
- Rate-limit respect.
- Platform policy review.
- Content owner approval.
- Brand/legal review where required.
- A visible audit trail of who approved what and when.

## Responsible statement

PostPilot OS supports planning, research, and education. It should never be used to deceive audiences, manipulate engagement, evade platform controls, or automate harmful outreach.
