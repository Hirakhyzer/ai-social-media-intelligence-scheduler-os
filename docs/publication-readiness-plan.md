# Publication Readiness Plan

PostPilot OS can be framed as a research artifact for ethical AI-assisted social-media planning and scheduling.

## Possible paper framing

**Working title:** PostPilot OS: A Simulation-First Operating System for Ethical Social Media Intelligence, Scheduling, and Analytics

The repository can support research questions such as:

1. Can a unified scheduling OS combine trend discovery, platform-fit scoring, content drafting, and analytics into one auditable workflow?
2. How can brand-safety gates reduce unsafe or misleading content recommendations before human review?
3. How do best-time recommendations and platform-fit scores affect synthetic campaign outcomes?
4. What audit information is needed before moving from simulation to real platform adapters?

## Suggested paper structure

1. Introduction and motivation.
2. Ethical marketing and platform-governance background.
3. System architecture.
4. Synthetic dataset design.
5. Trend radar and platform-fit scoring.
6. Scheduling and calendar generation.
7. Brand-safety guardrails and human approval gate.
8. Synthetic analytics and A/B testing.
9. Results and ablations.
10. Limitations and deployment boundary.

## Minimum artifact checklist

- [ ] Clean README and diagrams.
- [ ] Fixed-seed synthetic run.
- [ ] Reproducibility playbook.
- [ ] Ethical marketing boundary.
- [ ] Output report and figures.
- [ ] Audit ledger sample.
- [ ] Clear statement that no real posting occurs by default.
